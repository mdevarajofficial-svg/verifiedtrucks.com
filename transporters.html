If you searched for loads for truck, lorry loads, return load, or transporter login, start with Verify truck. Google sign-in is offered after your details are filled, and those details are stored on your profile.

What you submit

Full name, contact number, driving licence number, and total vehicles owned. That is identity for bidding — it is not a government fitness certificate issued by us.

Bidding

Open loads show pickup, drop, size, body type and tonnage — not the load party’s phone. Filter by size. Place a bid with your name, mobile, vehicle number and amount. You can edit the bid to try for #1. If someone quotes less, their amount shows first and yours second.

If the load party accepts your bid, you get a message that they will contact you. You still will not see their number.
