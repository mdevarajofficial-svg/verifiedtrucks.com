About Verified Trucks

Verified Trucks is an online marketplace to book a truck or hire a tempo / mini truck / lorry for goods movement in India. Load parties post pickup, drop, size and tonnage. Transporters who complete Verify truck can bid. We aim to match a vehicle in about 10 minutes; that is a search target, not a guarantee.
