Why choose Verified Trucks

Post a load, watch the 30-minute reverse timer, and take the lowest bid. Transporters bid from Verify truck or Login. You accept in Profile and get their phone and vehicle number.

01

Live 30-minute market

Each posted load stays on the board for 30 minutes with a reverse timer, then it disappears.

02

Lowest bid is the best bid

You see how many bids arrived and the lowest amount. Accept or reject from your Login profile.

03

Anyone can bid — except on their own load

Load posters and transporters use the same market. You cannot bid on a trip you posted.

04

Contact after you accept

When you accept a bid you get that bidder’s phone and vehicle number. They do not get yours.

Bilty, POD, invoices & e-way bill

Bilty, POD, invoice and e-way bill are typical for road freight. Confirm who will issue them before the vehicle starts.

Multiple payment options

Pay as you agree with the transporter on the accepted bid — often UPI or bank transfer.

Help when a booking is stuck

Use the number on your booking if you need help. Coverage is not a dedicated night desk in every city.

When more than one transporter bids, you see the lowest bid amount and can accept it. What you pay depends on the route, vehicle, and who is free — not a fixed discount.
