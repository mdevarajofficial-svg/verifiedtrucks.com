About Verified Trucks

Verified Trucks is a website to book a truck online in India and to help transporters find loads. We do not own a national fleet. We list your requirement, show matching vehicle types, and let verified transporters place bids. You choose whether to accept.

Who the platform is for

If you are a manufacturer, trader, shop, or household moving goods, you can hire a truck, book a mini truck, or request a tempo / Tata Ace / lorry for a point-to-point trip. If you own trucks, you can complete Verify truck, sign in, and bid on posted loads.

This is goods transport — pickup to drop — not passenger cabs or packers-and-movers full home shifting packages.

How online truck booking works here

  1. Enter loading and unloading locations, then size in feet, open or container body, vehicle model, tonnage, and your contact number.
  2. Submit the form. The load is saved in Firestore immediately. Your phone stays off the public bid board.
  3. Transporters bid. You see how many bids arrived and only the lowest bid amount.
  4. Accept that bid, then confirm the vehicle to get the transporter’s number. They are told you will call. They do not get your number.

The home page timer counts ten minutes while a match is sought. If no one is free, it keeps looking. That timer is a wait display, not a promise that a truck is always assigned in ten minutes.

Paperwork, payment, and support

Road freight in India often needs a bilty (consignment note), proof of delivery, invoice, and an e-way bill when the goods and value require it. After you confirm a vehicle, arrange those documents with the transporter as the law and your goods require. We do not replace GST, RTO, or e-way bill portals.

Payment is agreed on the bid you accept. Methods such as UPI or bank transfer are between you and the transporter unless a specific trip says otherwise.

If something is stuck on the website, use the contact you saved on the booking. Response times follow working hours plus whatever coverage we can staff; we do not claim a dedicated agent is always on a night shift for every city.

What we do not claim

  • We do not guarantee a truck in 10 minutes on every route or at every hour.
  • We do not guarantee the lowest rate in India — only the lowest bid placed on that load.
  • We do not guarantee GPS on every vehicle. Ask the transporter before you confirm.
  • We do not operate every city equally. Coverage is whoever is logged in and bidding.
  • We do not take over insurance, customs, or illegal or restricted cargo.

Common questions

Can I book a truck without calling a broker?

Yes. Fill the online form and wait for bids on your profile. You still call the transporter after you confirm the vehicle.

Do you offer part load and full truck load?

You post one load with size and tonnage. Whether a transporter treats it as part load or a full vehicle is their bid. We do not run a separate part-load consolidation warehouse.

Which cities are covered?

Any pickup and drop you type. A match happens only if a transporter bids. Large corridors are more likely to get bids than remote lanes at odd hours.

How do I get loads for my truck?

Open Verify truck. Live loads stay for 10 minutes. Bid with phone and vehicle number — no Google login.
