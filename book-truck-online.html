Book a truck online

Use this page if you searched for book truck online, hire truck, mini truck booking, tempo for goods, or lorry hire India. The actual form is on the home page. This page explains what you will be asked and what happens next.

What you enter

Loading location and unloading location first. Then trailer size in feet, open or container, a matching vehicle model (for example Tata Ace, Intra, Dost, 407, or a 32 ft container), tonnage, name and a 10-digit mobile. Questions appear one after another so you are not looking at a long form at once.

Open the booking form

Part load, full truck, and intercity trips

Intercity goods transport, city-to-city cargo, and same-state trips all use the same form. If your goods do not fill the vehicle, say so in tonnage and size; transporters will bid on that. We do not run a shared warehouse that consolidates many shippers into one lorry.

After you submit

You sign in with Google. The load sits on your profile with a countdown while transporters bid. You see bid count and the lowest amount only. Accept, then confirm the vehicle to get their contact. Assignment can be quick when vehicles are free nearby; it can also take longer or fail to find a bidder. The ten-minute display loops while we keep looking.
